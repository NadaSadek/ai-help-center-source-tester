# Invite team members

Workspace owners and admins can invite new team members from the workspace settings page.

Each invite is tied to an email address. To avoid access problems, make sure the invited user signs in with the same email address that received the invitation.

If a user says they cannot access the workspace, confirm whether they accepted the invite, used the correct email address and joined the intended workspace.

Inviting a team member does not automatically grant billing permissions. Billing settings, invoices and payment methods are still limited to workspace owners and billing admins.

If the user was invited successfully but paid features are still unavailable after a billing change, review entitlement sync information.
