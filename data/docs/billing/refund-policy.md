# Refund policy

Cancelling an ExampleOps plan does not automatically create a refund.

Refunds are reviewed based on the type of charge, the timing of the request and whether the paid plan was used during the billing period. Monthly subscriptions are usually not refunded after the billing period has started.

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact support with your workspace name, invoice number and the reason for the refund request.

Refund requests are separate from cancellation requests. Cancelling prevents future renewal charges, while a refund request asks ExampleOps to review a past charge.

If your team still has access after cancelling, that does not always mean a refund is owed. Access usually continues until the end of the paid billing period.
