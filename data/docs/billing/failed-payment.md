# Failed payment

If a payment fails, ExampleOps will show a billing warning to workspace owners and billing admins.

Common reasons for failed payments include expired cards, insufficient funds, bank declines, authentication requirements and payment method restrictions.

ExampleOps may retry the payment automatically. During the retry period, your workspace may continue to have access to paid features. If the invoice remains unpaid, access to some paid features may be restricted.

A failed payment is not the same as cancelling a plan. Cancellation is requested by a user and usually takes effect at the end of the billing period. Failed payment restrictions happen when an invoice cannot be collected.

If your invoice is marked as paid but your team still cannot access the workspace, check workspace access and entitlement sync information. The issue may not be a payment failure.
