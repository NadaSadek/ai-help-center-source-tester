# Access after cancellation

When a paid ExampleOps plan is cancelled, access usually continues until the end of the current billing period.

This means your team may still be able to use paid features after cancellation. This is expected behavior and does not mean the cancellation failed. The exact access end date is shown on the billing settings page.

At the end of the billing period, the workspace moves to the free plan. Features that are not included in the free plan may stop working. Existing data remains in the workspace, but some actions may be limited until the plan is upgraded again.

Access after cancellation is different from a failed payment restriction. A cancelled plan stays active until the scheduled end date. A failed payment may restrict access earlier if the invoice remains unpaid after retry attempts.
