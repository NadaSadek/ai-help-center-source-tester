# Billing permissions

Billing settings are only available to workspace owners and users with billing admin permissions.

Users with billing permissions can view invoices, update payment methods, change plans and cancel subscriptions. Regular members cannot manage billing settings unless their role is changed.

If the previous billing admin left the company, a workspace owner can assign billing permissions to another user. If no active owner is available, contact ExampleOps support for account recovery steps.

Billing permissions are separate from general workspace access. A user may be able to use the workspace but not manage billing. A user may also be a billing admin without being responsible for every workspace setting.

If your team is locked out after a payment issue, billing permissions alone may not fix the problem. Check failed payment, workspace access and entitlement sync information.
