# Download invoices

Workspace owners and billing admins can download invoices from the billing settings page.

Each invoice includes the billing period, amount charged, payment status, tax details and the payment method used. Paid invoices can be downloaded as PDF files.

If an invoice is marked as open or unpaid, the payment may still be processing or may have failed. Check the failed payment article for steps related to payment collection.

Invoice access depends on your workspace role. If you cannot see invoices, ask a workspace owner or billing admin to update your permissions.

Downloading an invoice does not change plan access, payment status or workspace permissions. If your team cannot access the workspace after payment, the issue may be related to workspace access or entitlement sync rather than the invoice itself.
