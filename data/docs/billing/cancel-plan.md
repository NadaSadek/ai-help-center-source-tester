# Cancel your plan

Workspace owners can cancel an ExampleOps subscription from the billing settings page.

When you cancel a paid plan, the cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period. Your workspace can usually continue using paid features until that date. The workspace is not deleted when the plan is cancelled.

After the billing period ends, the workspace moves to the free plan. Some paid features may become unavailable, including advanced exports, higher usage limits, premium integrations and some admin controls.

Cancelling a plan does not automatically issue a refund. Refund eligibility depends on the refund policy and the timing of the cancellation.

Only users with billing permissions can cancel a plan. If you cannot see the cancellation option, ask a workspace owner or billing admin to check your role.
