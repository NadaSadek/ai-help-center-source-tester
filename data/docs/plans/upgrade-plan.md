# Upgrade your plan

Workspace owners and billing admins can upgrade an ExampleOps plan from the plan settings page.

Upgrading can increase limits for projects, team members, exports, integrations and automation runs. Some advanced features are only available on higher plans.

When a plan is upgraded, the billing change is applied to the workspace and entitlements may take a short time to sync. If the invoice is paid but new limits are not available yet, review entitlement sync information.

Only users with billing permissions can upgrade a plan. Regular workspace members cannot change subscription level.

If your team needs more capacity because you reached a limit, upgrading may be the correct action. If you cannot access an existing workspace, review workspace access instead.
