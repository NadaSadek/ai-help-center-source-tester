# Plan limits

Each ExampleOps plan includes limits for workspaces, projects, team members, exports, integrations and automation runs.

If your team cannot add more projects, invite more members or create additional workflows, you may have reached a plan limit. The current limit and usage count are shown on the plan settings page.

Plan limits are different from billing access issues. A workspace can have an active paid plan and still be blocked from creating more resources if the current plan limit has been reached.

Upgrading increases available limits for future usage. Downgrading may reduce limits at the end of the billing period.

If a feature is unavailable after a payment or subscription change, review entitlement sync information as well as the plan limits page.
