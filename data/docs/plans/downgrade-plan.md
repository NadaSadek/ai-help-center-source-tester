# Downgrade your plan

Workspace owners and billing admins can downgrade an ExampleOps plan from the plan settings page.

A downgrade is usually scheduled for the end of the current billing period. Until then, the workspace may continue to use the current paid features and limits.

After the downgrade takes effect, features and limits from the higher plan may no longer be available. Existing data remains in the workspace, but some actions may be limited until the plan is upgraded again.

Downgrading a plan is different from cancelling a subscription. Cancellation moves the workspace to the free plan at the end of the billing period. Downgrading moves the workspace to a lower paid plan.

Downgrading does not automatically create a refund. Refund requests are reviewed separately.
